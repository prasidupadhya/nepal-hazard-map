# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their
age, body, ability, ethnicity, caste, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, appearance, religion, language, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to open source or writing
  in their second language.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Back claims about data with sources.
- Remember that this project is about disasters that hurt real communities:
  write about them with care.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spreading false hazard information, or presenting scenarios as real warnings.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@prasidupadhya](https://github.com/prasidupadhya)) may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may block people from the project temporarily or
permanently.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the
comment or issue, or **Report abuse** on the person's profile. Reports are
handled in confidence.
